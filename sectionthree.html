Are the People Getting Louder?

Along with conflicts and disputes between countries, our news are often filled with reports of protests, peaceful or not. Public demonstrations like those are a clear indicator of the public's opinion and dissent. Let's briefly see their evolution over the last few years.

Stable Conscription

The following plot shows the average population exposure for protests and similar events along the years, divided by regions.

We can see a somewhat common trend: the values greatly increase after 2020, possibly relating with the events of the time, and then tenuously decrease. The Asian region follows the same trend, but stands out among others for its superior entity, simply caused by its greater population.

All Quiet on the Protesting Front

The following plot analogously highlights the number of events per year.

The values follow a similar trend to the previous plot, with the number remaining relatively stable across all regions. The only element of note is the sudden increase in Europe and the Northern Americas after 2020, which mirrors the former chart.
