It comes in all shapes and forms

The map states how violence is expressed around the world, clearly highlighting where a certain type of event is the most recorded. In the so-called "western countries", we see how the majority of violence is represented by riots. On the other hand, in Asia seems like remote violence is the most used: this is coherent with the state of the conflicts present. The part of world mostly affected by violence against civilians is composed by regions where there are internal conflicts and civil wars.

A better look

We have seen before the comparison between three big conflicts in the world. But how are they spread across the countries? In the first map seems clear that the war is heavily present in Ukraine and immediately near the frontier. On the other hand, in Mexico the violence against civilians seems to prevail, and is distributed in the whole country. Lastly, in Israel and Palestine, we can see a higher presence of events in Gaza City and in West Bank: in the former, the highest number of events is defined by remote violence, while in the latter we have more riots.

Violence spins our heads

No matter what kind, or how many people it affects; no matter if it's justified or not, but violence is heavily distributed around the globe. The visualization helps us find the hotspots, based on the intensity of the color and the height of the spike.
